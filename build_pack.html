Wiring

Signals are drawn; power is shown as labelled nets. Every GND is common. Numbers in brackets are package pins.

Power budget

Six 28BYJ-48 draw about 200 mA each with two coils on: 1.2 A at 100% duty, about 0.7 A at the 60% hold. Add the ESP32 (0.25 A), BKA30D-R5 (0.04 A) and Hall latches (0.02 A): about 1.5 A peak. The 5 V 3 A supply leaves headroom.

Hold switch

The P-channel switch sits on the motors' 5 V, not their ground, so the ULN2003 logic ground stays solid. The datasheet's 59–118 mN·m friction torque hints the motors may hold unpowered; run the holding-torque test first and leave this out if they do.

Hall timing

MCP23017 #2 raises INTB on any station latch edge; the ESP32 timestamps the interrupt and reads INTCAP to see which station passed. The year and moon latches go straight to ESP32 interrupts. The machine turns one way, so each station always reports the same edge.

Logic levels

MCP23017s and Hall latches run at 3.3 V; ULN2003 inputs switch fine from 3.3 V. The gauge driver runs at 5 V, so its five inputs go through the 74AHCT245, whose TTL-level inputs accept 3.3 V.

USB and the supply

The 1N5819 between the 5 V bus and the ESP32's 5V pin keeps a plugged-in USB cable from back-feeding the motor supply, and the supply from back-feeding your computer.

Faceplate

Must be aluminium, never steel: the latches look through it at the magnets. Each station gets a 3.4 mm square window at 12 o'clock, r 20.5, with two M2 holes for the sensor board.
